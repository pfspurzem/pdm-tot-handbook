2.1.1 – Drawing Your Tree of Life

🔑 GETTING STARTED

Introduction

Like a Tree, we have aspects of our life that are growing and producing fruits. We rarely get to take a step back and look at our lives in this way.

📘 TREE OF LIFE INTRODUCTION

Like a Tree, we have aspects of our life that are growing and producing fruits.

But, we rarely get to take a step back and look at our lives in this way.

📝

Trees are a powerful symbol of “life”, with many interesting features…

  • We all appreciate when a mature tree bears fruits, and we appreciate what these fruits offer for other life around it.
  • I looked at a tree the other day and saw not only the fruits, but also the branches and buds, growing long before the next fruit arrives.
  • I see the center trunk that helps the tree stand tall, to support more branches and buds to grow.
  • I saw that the roots reached deep under ground, growing and being part of the tree ever since it was just a little seed.
  • It makes me think about my own life – my own roots and the fruits that I produce today. How tall can I grow, and how strong will my trunk need to be? What buds and fruits will I grow in the future?
  • Just like a tree, there are many interesting parts of your life to examine! That is exactly what we will do now.

👤 TREE OF LIFE: DRAW YOURS

Open the participant packets to the drawing of the tree, where you will write about your life.

📝

Tips before beginning:

  • Work one section at a time.
  • You can either write words, draw or just think about things.
  • For example in your roots, you might want to draw the house where you grew up as a child, or the face of a particular school teacher who supported you, etc.
💡

Draw Your Tree of Life

  1. Begin with the ROOTS for 2-minutes
  2. The roots represent your history and life experiences. This includes your family, friends, and community.
  3. Roots can include people, places, and experiences have shaped you—in both positive and negative ways.
  4. Focus on our TRUNK for two minutes:
  5. The TRUNK represents everything about your life today. What do you do with your days? Who or what are you nearby?
  6. What do you have around you that gives you support? What around you feels like it’s causing hardship or becoming a problem?
  7. Focus on our FRUITS: Two minutes to write or draw!
  8. The FRUITS represent your biggest accomplishments.
  9. These can be big things or small things – they just need to be important to you.
  10. What have you overcome in your past? What skills or equipment have you learned to use?
  11. Keep writing until you have at least 4-5 or more fruits!*
  12. Focus on our BUDS for a final two minutes:
  13. The BUDS represent your hopes and dreams for the future.
  14. What are the new things that you want to happen in your life?
  15. What fruits do you want to see in 5-years, 10-years? These buds should make you excited! Dream big.
  16. Keep writing and drawing until you have at least 4-5+ buds. b

2.1.2 – Sharing Tree of Life

👤 TREE OF LIFE: PAIR SHARING

Tree of Life Pair-sharing

Take about 15 minutes to share (7 minutes per person) and ensure both partners are able to share. You do not have to share everything. Only share what you want to! Ask participants not to comment on what their group members say as they are talking, but just listen actively.

🔑 CLOSING KEY MESSAGES

Closing Key Messages

Like a tree, we continue to grow. And like a tree, we can improve how well we grow by ensuring that we are supporting ourselves in the best ways possible. You can continue adding to your Tree of Life if you think of more things. Every person has a past, present, fruits, and ideas for their future. You can be curious and learn from others!
