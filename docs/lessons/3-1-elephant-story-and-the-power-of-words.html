3.1.1 – Power of Words

👤 “I am stupid / I am strong”

Experiment! “Power of Words”

We’ll do a short experiment now: To begin, please stand up Take a moment to notice how you feel right now. We’re going to say a 3-word statement together, for 10 seconds. The statement is: ‘I am stupid’ You will say this aloud 10 times: ‘I am stupid’, ‘I am stupid’, ‘I am stupid’, … Ready? Go! Now, how do you feel? Physically? Emotionally? Mentally? What did we sound like? How did we sound when we were saying these things? (likely quiet, down, uncomfortable) Now we’re going to change just one of the words in the statement and do it again. Now we will say: ‘I am strong’ Repeat the phrase together aloud 10 times: ‘I am strong, ‘I am strong, ‘I am strong, …” Ready? Go! How do you feel now? Was there any difference after changing the 1-word? Some people notice a difference. Some people don’t. We’re just trying to notice our think-feel-do cycle and the power of words to change our cycles sometimes.

Team notes & insights

🔑 CLOSING KEY MESSAGES

Closing Messages

Our words can often have a big impact on how we feel, so it’s worth paying attention to this, including what we say to ourselves!

Reflection

Often times our Limiting Beliefs today come from experiences in our past, or our Roots “Roots → Think-Feel-Do → Actions” What examples came up in your discussions? After 2-3 volunteers, thank everyone for their participation & curiosity. Then continue to the Elephant Story.

3.1.2 – Elephant Story (Pt 1) - Limiting Beliefs

📘 ELEPHANT STORY (PART 1: ROPES)

Share the story of the Elephant (part 1):

  1. Two gentlemen captured a small elephant and tied it to a wooden peg with a rope near their home.

  2. In the first month, the elephant pulled and pulled to get away. It hurt and he couldn’t break free.

  3. He continued to pull and pull, with no luck.

  4. By the third month, it stopped trying.

  5. Years later, the 100 kg baby is a 5,000 kg powerhouse. He is still kept on a rope, still connected to the wooden peg.

  6. It could walk away, but it doesn’t dare try.

  7. Two wild elephants passed by and said ‘You could break free easily’

  8. The elephant replied, “I would like to join you, but you don’t know how strong this rope is.” He didn’t listen to the other elephants' advice. He didn’t even think to ask for help.

  9. He looked longingly at the wild elephants and became sad, but then said to himself ‘this is just how my life is now, it will never change’

💬 Elephant Story Pt.1 - Debrief Discussion (3 mins)

  • What is the moral of the story?

  • What was the elephant’s hope and dream?

  • What are the facts and strengths that the elephant overlooked?

  • What are those ropes that are holding you or pulling you down today?

  • What excuses are people giving for not succeeding in life?

  • What are you going to do to break those ropes in your life, family or business?

  • What are the facts and strengths that you might be overlooking?

Team notes & insights

🔑 CLOSING KEY MESSAGES

Closing Messages

When we think about our BUDS from our Tree of Life (goals, hopes, dreams), oftentimes we can have negative thoughts that make us doubt ourselves or feel hopeless. These are limiting beliefs. Limiting beliefs can come in the form of excuses, negative thoughts, worries, justifications, past failures, fear, etc. At first it may be hard to recognize our limiting beliefs, and we may have trouble realizing that they are not truths! It is usually uncomfortable to look for our own limiting beliefs – but don’t worry, we will not leave you here stuck with your ropes. Next we will learn some ways to break free. The elephant’s story is not yet over…

3.1.3 – Elephant Story (Pt 2) - Reframes

📘 ELEPHANT STORY (PART 2: ROPES)

Share the story of the Elephant (part 2)

  • This image is also in the participant packet.

  • The elephant wasn’t happy. He wanted explore the forest, eat wild fruits and meet other elephants

  • Then the rains came and it saw its reflection in a pool of water. One voice in his head said ‘I am a captured elephant. There is nothing I can do’

  • He looked more closely at himself, he noticed that his tusks were thicker, his legs were bigger.

  • He had learned things over the years, like when his keeper leaves the home and which direction the wild elephants lived.

  • Suddenly, he thought, ‘What if I follow the path of the wild elephants?’

  • He pulled at the rope and nothing happened. He said to himself ‘don’t be foolish, you have tried this before and you failed. Now you have failed again’

  • He remembered his reflection and say to himself ‘I have grown, I have learned, I can try again’

  • This time he pulled from one side, and then another, then he used his tusks, and slowly the ropes were getting loose.

  • The next day, when the keeper was out, the elephant broke free and walked into the forest. He noticed some dark shadows and vines and got nervous again.

  • But then passing another pond he saw himself and remembered: ‘There may be new ropes ahead. But I am a different elephant now. Ropes won’t hold me forever’

💬 Questions for the group (after each story)

Story Debrief

What is the moral of the story? Hint: What are the facts and strengths that the elephant overlooked? What are those ropes that are holding you or pulling you down today? What are the facts and strengths that you might be overlooking that could help you break free?

Team notes & insights

🔑 CLOSING KEY MESSAGES

📝
  • You can shift your focus and reduce the power of your limiting beliefs / ‘ropes’.
  • To find a reframe: think about your qualities, strengths, or past achievements that make you feel more powerful to move towards the vision you have for your life and future.
  • Looking closer at your Tree of Life can reveal many strengths and experiences that give you power to move towards your hopes and dreams (buds).

Examples: LB → Reframe

Limiting Belief (Self-doubt) / Reframe (Positive Truth about You)

LIMITING BELIEF: Because I have children and don't have much money, I will never be able to go to university.

--> REFRAME: If it's important to me, I will keep looking for opportunities to learn. I can also take shorter courses that I'm able to afford.

LIMITING BELIEF: I can't reach my big savings goals.

--> REFRAME: I can save small amounts now and over time my savings will grow.

LIMITING BELIEF: I tried to start a business, but it was a failure. I don't want to fail again.

--> REFRAME: Every time I fail, I learn something new. It makes me stronger and wiser to try again.

LIMITING BELIEF: I am not smart enough to understand how to run a business.

--> REFRAME: I've learned to do many things, I can learn this too.

LIMITING BELIEF: I have nothing of value to give to others.

--> REFRAME: I have been helpful to other people in the past.

LIMITING BELIEF: I've never met any female electricians, so I won't be able to succeed in my electronics repair business.

--> REFRAME: Even though I haven't met other female electricians, I have the passion, skills and abilities to do the job well. I also have people I can ask for support when I need.
