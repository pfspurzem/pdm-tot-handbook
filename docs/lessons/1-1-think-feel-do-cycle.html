1.1.1 – Three Entrepreneurs, Three Results

Three Entrepreneurs Intro

Read the stories slowly one at a time, followed by questions.

📝

3 Entrepreneurs, 3 Results

I’m going to tell you a story about three entrepreneurs who get three different results…

Sarah, Moses, and Amina all sell chickens and eggs in the local market in different counties. One month, sales become very slow…

SARAH:

  • Sarah feels worried after noticing that sales are becoming slow…
  • Sarah spends her time thinking about how to improve her sales, leading her to feel more confident and hopeful that she can find a way forward.
  • She starts talking to customers and asking what they are looking for. Many customers mention children's clothes so she tries to change her product stock.
  • The following month, sales improve.

MOSES:

  • Moses feels worried after noticing that sales are becoming slow…
  • Moses spends a lot of time thinking and talking about how difficult business is.
  • He feels discouraged and complains about customers and competitors, which makes him feel more frustrated and upset.
  • A few weeks later, sales are still low.

AMINA:

  • Amina feels worried after noticing that sales are becoming slow…
  • Amina spends a lot of time worrying about making the wrong decision.
  • She keeps thinking about making a change to her business, but never decides exactly what to do.
  • A few weeks later, sales remain low.

3 Think-Feel-Do Cycles? 3 Results?

What differences do you notice between Sarah, Moses, Amina? Why do you think their results were different? What might have been happening inside the mind of each entrepreneur?

Team notes & insights

🔑 3 Entrepreneurs - CLOSING KEY MESSAGES

📝

CLOSING KEY MESSAGES

  • It’s natural to sometimes feel like giving up (like Moses) or know you should change something, but don’t actually do it (like Amina).
  • It’s important to be aware of how these thoughts and feelings are affecting our actions and ultimately our businesses.
  • The same situation can lead to different results depending on how we think, feel, and act.
  • Over the next few lessons, we will explore our own stories, beliefs, fears, and goals.

1.1.2 – Your Think-Feel-Do Cycles

💬 Intro to THINK-FEEL-DO CYCLES

📝

Think-Feel-Do Cycle

  • The way we think and the messages we tell ourselves are very powerful. What we think affects how we feel, and what we do.
  • This cycle continues all day for us, in many different situations – in a classroom, at the market, when sharing a meal with someone. We all are doing, thinking, and feeling, all of the time.
  • For example:
  • When I sleep very little, how do I FEEL in the morning? → Tired and frustrated!
  • When I FEEL angry and annoyed
