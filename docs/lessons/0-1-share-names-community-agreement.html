💬 TRAINER: COMMUNITY AGREEMENT

Background

When we all take part in creating the rules, then we feel more responsible for them. This is why we need YOU to help us determine the rules that YOU want to follow during your own preparation as a trainer.

Action Steps

Think about some rules of conduct for the training that appreciate and like others to follow.

📝

Some examples of rules of conduct include:

  • Respect one another
  • Silence our cell phones and minimize other distractions
  • Maintain confidentiality (what is shared in our group STAYS within our group (unless given permission to share outside)
  • Keep an open mind to new ideas and ways of thinking and sensing
💡

Assignment

Write THREE different rules that you would like us all to agree to.

Team notes & insights

💬 CREATING A COMMUNITY AGREEMENT

We will now co-create a community agreement.

  • A community agreement includes rules of conduct for the training.

  • Community agreements engage participants in the development of these ground rules so that participants take ownership of the rules and are responsible for them—individually and as a group.

ℹ️

In-Person Group Activity

On a flip chart, write “Community Agreement” at the top and ask participants some questions. Have participants share and write them on the flip chart as they share.

  • What are some good rules that we should set as a group for the space?
  • Is there anything you would like to take off this list, or anything else to add to it?

After giving the group an opportunity to suggest rules, proceed to group agreement:

  • Please raise your hand if you agree to this community agreement.

Once everyone has raised their hands, tape the community agreement to the wall in a place where people can see it over the course of the training.

  • Keep an open mind to new ideas and ways of thinking and sensing
Team notes & insights

🔑 CLOSING MESSAGES

📝

Reminders before we get started

  • We are all working together, and we are here to support each other.
  • All emotions are welcome—joy, anger, sadness, happiness, frustration, hope, and more.
  • Let yourself experience these emotions. The goal is to create and hold this safe space.
  • We will support you as you need us.

Example COMMUNITY AGREEMENT

💡 TRAINER TIP: Preparing for the community agreement

These can be especially important so we suggest you ensure that they are in the community agreements:

  • Commitment: Commit to being here for the full time of each session.

  • Punctuality: Arriving on time, so you can end on time.

  • Confidentiality: Personal issues and stories shared in the training room will not be shared outside of the training room.

  • Respect each other

  • Be open! (open to learn, open to re-think our assumptions)
