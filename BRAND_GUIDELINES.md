## Logo

- Wordmark: a small concentric-ripple/droplet icon + "SEE change" in mixed case (SEE all-caps, "change" lowercase).
- Variants: full color (blue wordmark, light droplet icon), all-navy, and reversed/white-on-black or white-on-dark-navy.
- Partner logo: Johns Hopkins Bloomberg School of Public Health shield + wordmark (color and reversed/white versions exist). Include SEE Change + JHU logos together in a footer when the page represents official program material.
- Logo source PNGs live in the same Drive branding folder as the fonts, not re-hosted here — reference by asking the user for exported PNGs/SVGs if the site needs to display them (nothing fetched into this repo automatically).

## Voice / brand traits

"We are: Accessible, Expert, Relevant, Useful"

- **Accessible** — comes from and connects with people from cultures around the world; values connection and nuance.
- **Expert** — combines and explores overlapping fields of research.
- **Relevant** — ideas and practices made relevant to individuals in their lives and circumstances.
- **Useful** — resources and research should be used; the best design is one partners love to use.

## Applying this to the lesson handbook site

- Headings (`h1`/`h2`) in Oswald Light, Dark Blue (`#21386D`) or Darkest Blue (`#0A263E`) on light backgrounds.
- Subheads (activity/block titles) in Quicksand Medium.
- Body text in Quicksand Regular, high-contrast dark gray/navy on white (not pure black, to match the deck's softer palette) — verify contrast ratio meets WCAG AA (4.5:1) against whatever background is used.
- Medium Blue / Light Blue for section backgrounds, callout boxes, or the top nav bar (matches the deck's own blue banner footer treatment).
- Accent colors (purple/red/yellow/green) reserved for small tags — e.g. labeling content type (Story, Activity, Knowledge Check) — not large color blocks, and never as the sole way to distinguish something (WCAG: pair color with text/icon).
- Print stylesheet: drop backgrounds/accent fills to save ink, keep Dark Blue only for headings, ensure body text renders as black or near-black on white for print contrast.
